Who we are

A pharmaceutical carton maker, from prepress to pallet.

Supreme Art is a Peshawar-based manufacturer of pharmaceutical folding cartons — the printed paper boxes that carry tablets, syrups, blisters and vials. We run the entire process in-house, so your artwork becomes a finished, machine-ready carton without ever leaving our floor.

  • Offset printing, die-cutting, folding & gluing in-house
  • Pharma-grade board with full batch traceability
  • Short runs through to full production volumes
