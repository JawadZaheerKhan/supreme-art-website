About us

A pharmaceutical carton maker, from prepress to pallet.

Supreme Art is a Peshawar-based manufacturer of pharmaceutical folding cartons — the printed paper boxes that carry tablets, syrups, blisters and vials. We run the entire process under one roof, so your artwork becomes a finished, machine-ready carton without leaving our floor.

For pharma brands, packaging is part of patient safety. That's why every job runs against documented quality controls, full batch traceability, and a team that has been printing for decades.

  • Offset printing, die-cutting, folding & gluing in-house
  • Pharma-grade board with batch traceability
  • Short runs to full production volumes
  • Prepress, dielines and physical proofing on site

Products & Capabilities

One factory. The whole carton.

From the first proof to the flat-packed, glued carton on your filling line — every stage happens here.

Offset Printing

Heidelberg Speedmaster presses for sharp, colour-accurate runs — from short batches to full production.

02

Die-Cutting & Creasing

Artwork checks, dielines and physical proofs before a single sheet runs.

Quality
