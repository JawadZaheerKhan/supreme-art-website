Our process

Quality checks at every stage.

1

Incoming board

Every batch of pharma-grade board is checked for grammage, thickness and consistency before it runs.

2

Colour control on press

Press-side colour management keeps branding accurate and consistent across the entire run.

3

Dimensional checks

Cartons are measured after die-cutting to confirm cuts and creases hold to the millimetre.

4

Final inspection

A final QA pass before dispatch, with batch records kept for full traceability.
