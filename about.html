Who we are

The paper box your medicine comes in — made under one roof.

Supreme Art (Pvt) Ltd is a Peshawar-based manufacturer of pharmaceutical folding cartons. From the first proof to the flat-packed, glued carton on your filling line, every stage of production happens in our own facility.

We work with pharmaceutical brands who need packaging that is accurate, consistent and traceable — because for medicine, the box is part of patient safety.
